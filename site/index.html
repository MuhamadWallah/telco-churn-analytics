Machine Learning Evaluation

4 Supervised Classification Models

Leakage-safe preprocessing pipeline with 80/20 train/test split (5,634 train / 1,409 test) and 5-fold Stratified Cross-Validation on the PostgreSQL warehouse.

Recall Focus (Random Forest): Best when missing a churner has severe business costs (e.g. enterprise accounts). Catches 289 out of 374 churners in the test holdout.
Precision Focus (Gradient Boosting): Best when retention offers/discounts are costly. Achieves 67.0% precision with minimum false alarms.
